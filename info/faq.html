Can I use your plugins on the Free version of DaVinci Resolve, or do I need Studio?

Check the description of each product. Blackmagic Design limits certain types of plugins to DaVinci Resolve Studio only, while others work in DaVinci Resolve Free as well.

Where are your other products?

Most legacy/free products are still available on Ko-Fi.

I purchased a product and I need to access it again

You can access purchases in the customer portal. If you purchased a legacy product, you can access it on Ko-Fi.

I installed a tool, but it isn't showing up, what do I do?

The most common causes of this issue are:

  • The installer failed to run because it was still inside of the zip file.
  • You have special characters in your username, so Fusion cannot write to it's configuration file.

If you're still having trouble, you can get email support or join the Discord server.

Can I sell my own presets that include your tools?

If you would like to release a preset for one of my tools, reach out about becoming an affiliate. To publish your own tool including one of my plugins you will need a redistribution/label license, contact me for pricing inquiries.

Can I use your tools in commercial projects?

Of course! Just make sure you aren't redistributing or sharing the tool itself. For more information please see the End User License Agreement.

What is your refund policy?

I accept most refund requests within one week of the purchase.
