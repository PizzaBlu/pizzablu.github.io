This Privacy Policy explains how PizzaBluFX ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit pizzablufx.com, purchase or use our products.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We may receive information that you provide when you:

  • Purchase one of our products.
  • Contact us for customer support.
  • Subscribe to or otherwise communicate with us through our website.

This information may include your name, email address, and other information you choose to provide.

Purchase Information

Purchases of our products are processed through Fourthwall or Creem, which acts as the merchant of record for our transactions.

Our merchant of record may collect information necessary to process your purchase, including payment and billing information. We do not directly collect or store your full payment card information.

We may receive limited purchase information from Creem, such as your name, email address, product purchased, transaction information, and information necessary to provide access to your purchased product.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically, including information about your browser, device, approximate geographic location, pages visited, and interactions with our website.

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website and to improve our products and website.

Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies to collect this information.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information we receive to:

  • Process and manage purchases.
  • Provide access to purchased digital products.
  • Provide product updates and related services.
  • Respond to customer support requests.
  • Understand how our website is used.
  • Improve our website, products, and services.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security issues, and technical problems.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to operate our website and business.

Creem

We use Creem to process purchases and provide merchant-of-record services. Creem may collect and process personal information in connection with purchases.

For more information, please see Creem's privacy policy.

Fourthwall

We use Fourthwall to process purchases and provide merchant-of-record services. Fourthwall may collect and process personal information in connection with purchases.

For more information, please see Fourthwall's privacy policy.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to analyze website traffic and usage. Google Analytics may collect information through cookies and similar technologies.

Google provides additional information about how it handles data in its privacy documentation.

We do not use Google Analytics to intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies.

We primarily use these technologies for website analytics and functionality. You can configure your browser to refuse or delete cookies. Disabling cookies may affect certain website functionality.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing products and support, maintaining business and transaction records, resolving disputes, and complying with legal obligations.

Third-party services we use may retain information according to their own privacy policies and retention practices.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our business, including payment and merchant-of-record providers, analytics providers, hosting providers, and other services necessary to provide our products and website.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, or services.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we maintain from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain uses of your information.

To exercise applicable privacy rights or ask questions about your personal information, contact us using the information below.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and products are not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Your continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy to the extent permitted by law.
